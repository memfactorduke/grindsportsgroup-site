About Us

Who We Are

Grind Sports Group is an athlete-first NIL agency specializing in connecting college football and basketball athletes with brands that align with their identity — creating partnerships that last beyond the season.

Our mission is to empower athletes by securing NIL deals and brand partnerships that authentically reflect their personal brands, passions, and long-term goals. We're dedicated to elevating athletic excellence through meaningful collaborations that support who our athletes are, on and off the field.

Meet The Founder

Jack Yates

Co-Founder · Duke University

Jack Yates is a Division I football player at Duke University and the Co-Founder of Grind Sports Group, where he helps college and professional athletes build authentic brand partnerships and expand their presence beyond sports.

Through his work in sports marketing, NIL, and athlete representation, he has helped facilitate collaborations with brands across wellness, recovery, apparel, hospitality, restaurants, and performance-focused industries.

In addition to competing at the Division I level, Jack has gained experience across multiple areas of the sports business industry through internships and hands-on work with professional sports organizations, athlete agencies, and marketing teams.

Jack is passionate about the business side of sports, relationship-building, and creating meaningful opportunities for athletes and brands alike. His long-term goal is to continue growing within the sports industry and eventually build a leading agency focused on innovative athlete marketing and representation.
